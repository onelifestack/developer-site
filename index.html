How we build

The same promises in every app

  • No account

    You never sign up or sign in. Our apps open straight to what they do.

  • Your data stays on your phone

    What you put in an app is stored on your device. We don't run a server that receives it.

  • No ads, no trackers

    No advertising SDKs, no analytics, no crash reporters that phone home.

  • Backups you hold

    Every app can export your data to a file you choose, and read it back in.

  • Few permissions

    We ask only for what a feature needs, and only when you use that feature.

  • One family of apps

    All our apps are signed with one key, so they can work together on your phone without a cloud in between.

About

Who we are

OneLifeStack is made by Omprakash Gautam, an independent developer working on a set of small personal apps under one idea: your story, connected, and kept by you. The Android apps are companions to onelifestack.com. Each one is useful on its own and none needs the others.

We answer email ourselves. Bug reports, ideas and questions about privacy are all welcome.
