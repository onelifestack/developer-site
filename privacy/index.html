Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy covers this website and how OneLifeStack handles email you send us. Each app has its own policy, written in plain words:

  • Pocketful privacy policy
  • Lots privacy policy

In short, our apps keep your data on your phone. They have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not receive what you put in them.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and loads nothing from other companies. Fonts and images are served from this domain.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running; see the GitHub privacy statement. We do not see those records.

Email

If you email us, we receive your address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to fix what you reported. We don't add you to a mailing list, sell your details or share them with anyone. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

Google Play

When you install one of our apps from Google Play, Google handles the download and any purchase under its own terms. Google gives developers summary statistics such as install counts and crash reports that you allowed Android to share; these do not identify you to us.

Children

Our apps and this site do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
